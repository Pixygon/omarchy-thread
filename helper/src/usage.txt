omarchy-thread — spatial rooms on the Thread, from your bar

  new <name>            make a room you own (a directory with a world.json)
  rooms                 list your rooms
  open [room|thread://…] serve your room and step inside — or walk to
                        any address on the Thread
  invite [room]         the address to hand someone
  status                one line of JSON — what the bar reads
  serve [room]          serve + watch, in the foreground (the daemon)
  stop                  close the room
  publish <host> [room] stage the room for a domain you own
  verify <host>         check a live host the way a browser does
  doctor                what's installed, what's reachable
  passport [status|set <token>|clear]
                        the portable "you" the browser already reads
  handler [status|install|remove]
                        make thread:// links open on this desktop

A room is a directory. Publishing is three files. Nothing here is
Pixygon-only: the format, the resolution rule and the presence wire are
the open Thread spec — https://github.com/Pixygon/thread-spec
